# Customer Communication Guidelines

How support and success teams write to customers.

## Tone

Professional, direct, and specific. State what happens next and when.
Avoid promising outcomes that policy does not guarantee.

## Commitments

Agents only commit to timelines that appear in approved service
documents. When a customer asks for something outside policy, the
agent explains the policy and offers the complaint or escalation
path rather than improvising an exception.

## Language

Customer-facing replies use plain language, name the relevant policy
when declining a request, and always include the next step available
to the customer.
