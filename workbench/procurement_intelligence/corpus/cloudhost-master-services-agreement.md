# CloudHost Master Services Agreement

Approved agreement terms for the CloudHost hosting service.

## Term and renewal

This agreement terminates on 2026-08-15. The agreement renews
automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party
gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the
termination date.

## Payment

Invoices under this agreement are payable within 21 days of the
invoice date. Late payment
accrues interest at the statutory rate.

## Service scope

CloudHost operates the production hosting environment described in the
service schedule, including managed backups and restore testing.
