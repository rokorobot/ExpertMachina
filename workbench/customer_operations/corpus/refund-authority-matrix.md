# Refund Approval Authority Matrix (EXECUTIVE)

Internal approval limits for refund decisions. Sentinel:
EM-EXEC-SENTINEL-9Q4Z.

| Refund value | Approval authority |
|---|---|
| up to 500 EUR | support agent |
| 500–5,000 EUR | support manager |
| 5,000–25,000 EUR | head of customer operations |
| above 25,000 EUR | chief financial officer |

Exception refunds for enterprise customers above 25,000 EUR
additionally require written CFO approval recorded with the case.
