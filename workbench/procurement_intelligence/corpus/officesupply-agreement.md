# OfficeSupply Framework Agreement

Approved framework agreement for office consumables.

## Term

This agreement terminates on 2027-09-30 and may be renewed by mutual
written agreement.

## Payment

Invoices under this agreement are payable within 60 days of the
invoice date.

## Contact and administration

Orders are placed against the framework through the supplier portal.
The registered office of the supplier is 4501 Commerce Park, Suite
210, and the account desk is reachable at +1 555 0142 during business
hours. Delivery disputes follow the escalation steps in clause 12.3,
quoting purchase order references in the 700000 number range.
