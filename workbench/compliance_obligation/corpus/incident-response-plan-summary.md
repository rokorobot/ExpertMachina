# Incident Response Plan — Summary

Approved summary of the incident response arrangements.

## Activation

A suspected security incident is reported to the on-call responder,
who classifies severity and activates the response bridge for high
severity events.

## Testing requirement

The incident response plan must be tested at least once during each
calendar year. Test scope alternates between a technical restore
exercise and a management tabletop exercise.

## Lessons learned

Post-incident reviews capture root cause, timeline, and follow-up
actions, and feed updates back into the plan.
