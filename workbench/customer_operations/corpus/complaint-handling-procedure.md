# Complaint Handling Procedure

Internal procedure for formal customer complaints (distinct from
support incidents).

## Intake

A complaint is registered when a customer expresses dissatisfaction
with the service, a decision, or an interaction and asks for it to be
reviewed. The receiving agent records the complaint with the customer
account, the subject, and the desired outcome.

## Handling

1. The shift lead assigns a complaint owner within 1 business day.
2. The owner acknowledges the complaint to the customer within 2
   business days.
3. The owner investigates, consults the relevant policy, and proposes
   a resolution within 10 business days.
4. Resolutions that grant exceptions to policy require support
   manager approval.

## Closure

The outcome and its reasoning are recorded and communicated to the
customer through an approved channel.
