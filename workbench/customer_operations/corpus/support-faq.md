# Customer Support FAQ

Answers support agents may share with customers.

## Refunds

Q: How long do customers have to request a refund?
A: Our refund policy allows customers to request a refund within 30
days of delivery. After that, refunds are only possible under a valid
warranty claim.

Q: How fast are refunds paid out?
A: Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method
within 10 business days.

## Orders

Q: Can a customer change a shipping address after ordering?
A: Yes, until the order status shows "dispatched". After dispatch,
the carrier's redirection service applies.

## Warranty

Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: Manufacturing defects for 24 months from delivery. Wear and tear
and accidental damage are not covered.
