# Customer Success — Approved Standard Terms

The governed baseline for customer service agreements. Every
customer-specific agreement is measured against these approved
standard terms; deviation is diagnosable only against this document
(the baseline doctrine: no standard baseline, no deviation
diagnosis).

## Service performance reporting

Under the approved standard terms, the provider shall deliver a
monthly service performance report to the customer within five
business days of the end of each calendar month.

## Term and renewal

Under the approved standard terms, every customer agreement runs for
twelve months and renews for successive twelve-month terms unless
either party gives notice at least ninety days before the end of the
current term.

## Escalations

Under the approved standard terms, customer escalations must follow
the approved support escalation procedure.
