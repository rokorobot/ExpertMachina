06 / Security Boundary

Knowledge Boundary

Your company knowledge stays inside your company boundary.

ExpertMachina is designed for organizations that cannot send sensitive internal knowledge into public AI tools. Your SOPs, client records, protocols, decision logs, emails, and operational documents are not uploaded to the open web or exposed to external generic LLM workflows. The system can be deployed as a private, permission-aware knowledge layer where access, retrieval, answers, and audit logs remain under company control.

05.01

Private Knowledge Vault

Internal documents stay secure inside controlled and compliant infrastructure environments without third-party leaks.

05.02

No Public Model Training

Your institutional data is never used to train public LLM models or accessed by generic consumer AI agents.

05.03

Permission-Gated Answers

Access control rules enforce that employees only receive retrieval queries they are authorized to read.

05.04

Auditable Access Logs
