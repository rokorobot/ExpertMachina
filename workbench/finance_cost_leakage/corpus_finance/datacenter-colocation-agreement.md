# Datacenter Colocation Agreement

Approved vendor agreement for datacenter colocation services.

## Fees

The customer shall pay a monthly colocation fee of 12,000 EUR,
payable within 30 days of the invoice date. The fee shall increase by
8% on each anniversary of the effective date, applied to the
then-current fee.

## Late payment penalty

Any amount not paid by its due date shall be subject to a
late-payment surcharge of 1.5% per month on the overdue balance,
charged until the amount is settled.

## Minimum commitment

The customer must maintain a minimum annual spend of 120,000 EUR
under this agreement. Amounts below the minimum commitment are
non-refundable and shall be charged as a true-up at the end of each
contract year.

## Renewal

This agreement shall renew automatically for successive one-year
terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at
least 90 days before the anniversary of the effective date.
