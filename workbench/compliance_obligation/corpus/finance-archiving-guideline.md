# Finance Archiving Guideline

Approved archiving practice for the finance department's document
store.

## Working papers

Working papers and drafts are archived at quarter close and reviewed
at year end for continued usefulness.

## Disposal of customer agreements

Customer contract records must be destroyed three years after
contract termination. Destruction is logged in the archive register
by the finance administrator.

## Storage

Archived material lives in the finance archive space with access
limited to the finance group.
