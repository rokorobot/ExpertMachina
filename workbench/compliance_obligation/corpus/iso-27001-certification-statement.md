# ISO 27001 Certification Statement

Approved statement of the company's certification commitments.

## Certification commitment

The company shall maintain certification of its information security
management system under ISO 27001. The certification scope covers the
hosted service and its supporting infrastructure.

## Surveillance audits

The Compliance Officer is responsible for coordinating the annual ISO
27001 surveillance audit and for retaining the certification
evidence. Surveillance findings are tracked to closure through the
management review.

## Scope statements

The current certificate and its scope statement are available to
customers under a signed confidentiality agreement.
