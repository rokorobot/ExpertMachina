# Support Escalation Procedure

Internal procedure for escalating customer support incidents.

## Severity levels

- SEV-1: service unavailable for multiple customers.
- SEV-2: service degraded, workaround exists.
- SEV-3: single-customer issue, no service impact.

## Enterprise incidents

The first-response target for enterprise incidents is 48 hours. The
assigned agent records the incident severity, informs the account
contact, and engages the on-duty engineer for SEV-1 and SEV-2.

## Escalation chain

1. Support agent → shift lead (any severity, agent judgment).
2. Shift lead → support manager (SEV-2 and above, or customer
   requests escalation).
3. Support manager → head of customer operations (SEV-1, or any
   incident open longer than 5 business days).

## Records

Every escalation is recorded with the incident reference, severity,
and the person who accepted the escalation.
