# Change Management SOP

Meridian Operations Ltd — Standard Operating Procedure OPS-CM-03.
This SOP describes how the platform team executes production changes.
All content is illustrative and fictional.

## Purpose

This SOP defines the operational steps the platform team follows to
deploy a production change.

## Process steps

Step 1. The engineer raises a change ticket describing the production
change. On submission the change ticket is routed to the team lead for
approval.

Step 2. The team lead reviews the change ticket. A production change
may be approved by the team lead alone before it is deployed. When the
team lead approves, the change is scheduled for deployment.

Step 3. The platform team deploys the scheduled change. After
deployment the change ticket is marked deployed.

Step 4. The platform team records the deployment outcome. Once the
outcome is recorded the change ticket is closed.
