# Support Macro — Password Reset

Approved reply text for password reset requests.

---

Hello {customer_name},

To reset your password, open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot
password". A reset link valid for 60 minutes will be sent to your
registered email address.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, please check your
spam folder. For security reasons, support agents can never see or
set your password directly.

Kind regards,
{agent_name}

---

Usage notes: verify the requester through the registered account
email before sending. Never reset credentials over the phone.
