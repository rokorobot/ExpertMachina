# Regulatory Record-Keeping Summary

Approved summary of record-keeping duties under the applicable
commercial regulations.

## Contract records

Customer contract records must be retained for ten years after
contract termination. Retention applies to the signed agreement, its
amendments, and the acceptance correspondence.

## Invoicing records

Issued invoices must be retained for the period the applicable tax
regulation prescribes. The finance team keeps the authoritative
retention schedule for tax documents.

## Access

Retained records are produced to a competent authority on a lawful
request through the legal team.
