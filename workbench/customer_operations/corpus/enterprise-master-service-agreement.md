# Enterprise Master Service Agreement — Service Terms (Template)

Approved template terms for enterprise service agreements.

## Service levels

The provider maintains the service with a monthly availability target
of 99.5% measured per calendar month, excluding announced maintenance
windows.

## Reporting obligation

The provider shall deliver a monthly service performance report to
the customer within five business days of the end of each calendar
month. The report covers availability, incident summary, and open
actions.

## Term and renewal

The agreement runs for twelve months and renews for successive
twelve-month terms unless either party gives notice ninety days
before the end of the current term.

## Data handling

Customer data is processed only inside the contracted service and is
not shared with third parties except as required to deliver the
service.
