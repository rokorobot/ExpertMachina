# Code of Business Conduct

Approved expectations for how the company does business.

## Fair dealing

We compete on the merits of our products and describe them
truthfully. Gifts and hospitality follow the published thresholds.

## Records honesty

Business records reflect the underlying transactions accurately and
are never altered to misstate an outcome.

## Housekeeping

Teams are encouraged to archive completed project files when
convenient. Tidy workspaces make handovers easier but are not a
formal duty.

## Speaking up

Concerns are raised through any manager or the confidential reporting
channel without fear of retaliation.
