# Change Management Policy

Meridian Operations Ltd — Governing Policy POL-CM-01.
This policy governs how production changes are authorized at Meridian.
All content is illustrative and fictional.

## Authorization requirement

Every production change must be approved by the Change Advisory Board
before it is deployed. No production change may be deployed on the
authority of a single approver.

## Emergency changes

An emergency change may be deployed before Change Advisory Board review
only when a Priority-1 incident is active, and it must be reviewed by
the Change Advisory Board within 1 business day after deployment.

## Review cadence

This policy must be reviewed at least once every 12 months. The last
review was recorded on 2024-01-15.
