# Information Security Policy

The approved top-level security policy.

## Commitment

The company protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability
of the information it holds for itself and its customers.

## Organization

Security responsibilities are delegated through the management system
documentation. Supporting standards cover access, acceptable use,
training, and incident response.

## Encryption

Customer data at rest and in transit must be protected with
encryption configured to the current cryptographic standard.

## Exceptions

Deviations from security standards require a documented risk
acceptance approved at the appropriate management level.
