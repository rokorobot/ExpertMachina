# Access Control Policy

Approved rules for granting and removing access to company systems.

## Granting access

Access to production systems must be granted through the approved
access request workflow with a named business justification. Standing
administrative access is limited to the platform team.

## Removing access

Leaver access is removed on the leaving date as part of the offboarding
checklist. Dormant accounts are disabled after ninety days without a
sign-in.

## Review discipline

This policy must be reviewed every 12 months, and its last completed
review was dated 2025-11-10. Review outcomes are recorded in the
policy history.
