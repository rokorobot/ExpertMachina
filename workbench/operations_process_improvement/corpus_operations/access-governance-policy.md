# Access Governance Policy

Meridian Operations Ltd — Governing Policy POL-AG-02.
This policy supersedes the Remote Access SOP (OPS-RA-04) remote access
rule. All content is illustrative and fictional.

## Supersession notice

This policy supersedes the shared-credential remote access rule stated
in the Remote Access SOP (OPS-RA-04). Where the two documents differ,
this policy governs.

## Remote access rule

Remote access to Meridian systems is granted using a personal
individually issued VPN credential with multi-factor authentication.
Shared team VPN credentials are prohibited. Each staff member is issued
a personal individually issued VPN credential by the IT team.

## Review cadence

This policy must be reviewed at least once every 12 months. The last
review was recorded on 2026-05-01.
