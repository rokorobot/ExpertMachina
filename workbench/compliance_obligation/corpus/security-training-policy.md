# Security Training Policy

Approved training requirements for all staff.

## Annual training

All employees must complete security awareness training during each
calendar year. New joiners complete the module inside their first
sixty days.

## Records

A completion summary for each training cycle shall be recorded and
retained for audit. The summary lists the cycle, the headcount in
scope, and the completion rate.

## Refreshers

Additional short refreshers follow significant incidents or material
changes to the threat picture, as announced by the security team.
