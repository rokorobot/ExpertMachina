# Standard Service Level — Tier 1 Support

Service levels for standard (non-enterprise) subscriptions.

## First response

Tier 1 support requests receive a first response within 4 business
hours during support hours (Mon–Fri, 08:00–18:00 CET).

## Resolution targets

- SEV-2: workaround within 2 business days.
- SEV-3: resolution or scheduled fix within 10 business days.

## Channels

Tier 1 support is available through the customer portal and email.
Phone support is an enterprise feature.
