# CloudHost SLA Schedule

Approved service level schedule under the CloudHost agreement.

## Availability

CloudHost shall provide 99.9% monthly service availability for the
production environment, measured at the platform boundary.

## Data access

CloudHost shall notify the customer in writing before granting any
subprocessor access to personal data held in the service.

## Support

Severity-one incidents receive a response within the window stated in
the support annex.
