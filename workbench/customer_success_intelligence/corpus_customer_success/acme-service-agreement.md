# Acme Industrial — Service Agreement (Customer-Specific Terms)

Customer-specific agreement terms for Acme Industrial. Where this
agreement states a term, it applies in place of the approved standard
terms.

## Service performance reporting

Under this agreement, the provider shall deliver a weekly service
performance report to Acme Industrial within two business days of the
end of each calendar week.

## Term and renewal

This agreement renews for successive twelve-month terms unless Acme
Industrial or the provider gives notice at least sixty days before
the end of the current term.

The current term of this agreement ends on 2026-09-30, and any notice
of non-renewal must be given at least sixty days before that date.

## Quarterly business review

Under this agreement, the provider shall conduct a quarterly business
review with Acme Industrial during each contract quarter.

## Maintenance communication

Acme Industrial must be notified of planned maintenance windows at
least ten business days in advance under this agreement.

## Escalations

Escalations raised by Acme Industrial must follow the provider's
approved support escalation procedure.
