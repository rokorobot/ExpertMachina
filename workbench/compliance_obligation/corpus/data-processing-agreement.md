# Data Processing Agreement — Processor Obligations (Template)

Approved template terms governing personal data processing performed
for customers.

## Processing scope

The processor processes personal data only on documented instructions
from the customer and only for delivering the contracted service.

## Breach notification

The processor must notify the customer's data protection contact in
writing of any personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware
of the breach. The notice describes the nature of the breach, the
categories affected, and the measures taken.

## Sub-processors

The processor shall not engage a new sub-processor without the
customer's prior written approval. A current sub-processor list is
made available on request.

## Assistance

The processor provides reasonable assistance with data subject
requests and with regulatory inquiries relating to the contracted
processing.
