# Procurement Policy

The approved company policy for vendor engagement and purchasing.

## Payment terms

Payment terms in vendor agreements must be at least 45 days from the
invoice date. Shorter terms require a documented exception approved by
the finance director.

## Supplier certifications

Every supplier that processes customer data must hold a current ISO
27001 certificate, retained in the supplier file and refreshed at each
certification cycle.

## Engagement

New vendor engagements above the published threshold follow the
approved sourcing process with a named business justification.
