# Enterprise Service Overview — Customer Commitments

What enterprise customers can expect from our service organization.

## Support commitment

Enterprise customers are guaranteed a first response within 24 hours,
every day of the year. Enterprise customers receive a named account
contact and priority routing on all channels.

## Service quality

Enterprise subscriptions include quarterly business reviews, priority
access to maintenance windows, and early notification of planned
changes.

## Flexibility

Enterprise agreements support custom terms, including extended refund
consideration and tailored service levels, as agreed in the master
service agreement.
