# Northwind Logistics — Service Agreement (Customer-Specific Terms)

Customer-specific agreement terms for Northwind Logistics. This
agreement follows the approved standard terms.

## Service performance reporting

Under this agreement, the provider shall deliver a monthly service
performance report to Northwind Logistics within five business days
of the end of each calendar month.

## Term and renewal

This agreement renews for successive twelve-month terms unless
Northwind Logistics or the provider gives notice at least ninety days
before the end of the current term.

The current term of this agreement ends on 2027-03-31, and any notice
of non-renewal must be given at least ninety days before that date.

## Escalations

Escalations raised by Northwind Logistics must follow the provider's
approved support escalation procedure.
