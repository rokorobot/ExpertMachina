# Finance Policy

Approved company finance policy governing spend approval, payment
discipline, and budget control.

## Spend approval thresholds

Any spend commitment must be approved before the obligation is
entered. Commitments up to 5,000 EUR may be approved by a department
manager. Commitments above 5,000 EUR and up to 50,000 EUR must be
approved by the finance director. Commitments above 50,000 EUR must be
approved by the chief financial officer.

## Payment terms discipline

Payment terms in vendor agreements must be at least 30 days from the
invoice date. Shorter terms require a documented exception approved by
the finance director.

## Budget discipline

Every recurring cost commitment must be recorded against an approved
budget line before renewal. A renewal that increases the annual cost
by more than 10% must be re-approved by the finance director before
the renewal date.

## Price-increase review

Any vendor price increase stated in a contract clause must be reviewed
by the finance team against the approved budget line before it takes
effect.
