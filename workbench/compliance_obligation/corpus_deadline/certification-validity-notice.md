# Certification Validity Notice

Approved statement of the current certification validity and the
recertification scheduling duty.

## Certificate validity

The company must maintain its ISO 27001 certificate, which is valid
until 2026-11-30, under the certified scope. The certificate scope
statement is unchanged from the current issued certificate.

## Recertification scheduling

The recertification audit must be scheduled at least 90 days before
the certificate expiry date. The Compliance Officer must confirm the
audit booking in writing to the executive sponsor.

## Evidence retention

Certification evidence packages must be retained for each audit
cycle under the record-keeping requirements already in force.
