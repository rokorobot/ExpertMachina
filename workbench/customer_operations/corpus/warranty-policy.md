# Warranty Policy

## Coverage

Products carry a 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects,
counted from the delivery date. Wear and tear, accidental damage, and
unauthorized modifications are not covered.

## Claims

Warranty claims are submitted through the customer portal with the
order reference and a description of the defect. The support team
confirms coverage within 5 business days and arranges repair,
replacement, or refund at the company's discretion.

## Relation to refunds

A valid warranty claim may lead to a refund outside the standard
refund window; the warranty assessment governs in that case.
