# Vendor Notification Guideline

Approved guideline for vendor-facing notification duties.

## Incident notification

Vendors must notify the security team promptly upon discovering a
security incident affecting company data. The notification must
include the affected systems and the vendor's initial assessment.

## Record archival

Vendor engagement records must be archived within a reasonable period
after the end of each engagement. The archived records must include
the signed engagement summary.

## Escalation contact

Vendors must maintain a current escalation contact with the vendor
management office and must update it in a timely manner when the
contact changes.
