# Refund Policy

Effective for all consumer and business customers.

## Standard refund window

Refund policy: customers may request a refund within 30 days of
delivery. Refund requests received after the standard window are
declined unless a warranty claim applies.

## How to request

Refund requests are submitted through the customer portal or by
contacting the support team through an approved contact channel. The
support agent verifies the order, the delivery date, and the product
condition before approving a standard refund.

## Payment

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within
10 business days of approval.
