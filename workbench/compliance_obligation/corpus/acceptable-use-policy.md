# Acceptable Use Policy

Approved rules for using company systems and equipment.

## Scope

The policy applies to every person using company-managed devices,
accounts, or networks, including contractors.

## Core rules

Company systems are used for business purposes; incidental personal
use is tolerated where it does not interfere with work or security.
Credentials must never be shared between individuals.

## Review discipline

This policy must be reviewed every 12 months, and its last completed
review was dated 2024-05-02. Review outcomes are recorded in the
policy history.
